\subsection{Emergency Safety Fault Handling and Context Recovery}

Figure~\ref{fig:flow-fault} illustrates the fail-safe emergency shutdown procedure and context-restoring
recovery path governed by Safety Invariant $I_3$ and Assumption A8.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \resizebox{0.90\linewidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=0.55cm]
    \node[fcterm, fill=red!15] (fault) {\code{WM_EVT_FAULT_OCCURRED} Asserted};
    \node[fcproc, below=of fault] (save) {Snapshot Active State: $X_{\text{saved}} \gets S$\\Record Error Flags: $f \gets \text{flags}$};
    \node[fcproc, below=of save] (safe) {Immediate Fail-Safe Shutdown ($T_{\text{react}} \le 30\,\text{ms}$):\\Motor $\gets$ \code{OFF}, Pump $\gets$ \code{OFF}, Valve $\gets$ \code{CLOSED},\\Door Deadbolt $\gets$ \code{UNLOCKED}, RLED $\gets$ \code{BLINK 2Hz}};
    \node[fcproc, below=of safe] (enter) {Enter \st{ERROR} State\\Lock Out User Command Inputs};
    \node[fcdec, below=of enter] (wait) {\code{WM_EVT_FAULT_CLEARED}?};
    \node[fcdec, below=1.0cm of wait] (was_cycle) {$X_{\text{saved}} \in \{\text{\st{RUNNING}}, \text{\st{PAUSED}}\}$\\$\land\; t > 0$?};
    \node[fcproc, below left=1.0cm and -0.4cm of was_cycle] (res_pause) {Transition to \st{PAUSED}\\Actuators Remain Suspended\\BLED $\gets$ Solid \code{ON}\\Door $\gets$ \code{LOCKED}\\Awaiting \code{RUN} to Resume};
    \node[fcproc, below right=1.0cm and -0.4cm of was_cycle] (res_idle) {Restore Idle State by Balance:\\$b \ge 50 \implies \text{\st{READY}}$\\$b > 0 \implies \text{\st{COLLECTING}}$\\$b = 0 \implies \text{\st{STANDBY}}$};

    \draw[fa] (fault) -- (save);
    \draw[fa] (save) -- (safe);
    \draw[fa] (safe) -- (enter);
    \draw[fa] (enter) -- (wait);
    \draw[fa] (wait) -- node[right, font=\scriptsize]{yes} (was_cycle);
    \draw[fa] (wait.east) -- ++(2.8,0) node[pos=0.45, above, font=\scriptsize]{no (TICK$_{1\text{s}}$ active)} |- (enter.east);
    \draw[fa] (was_cycle) -| node[above, font=\scriptsize, pos=0.25]{yes} (res_pause);
    \draw[fa] (was_cycle) -| node[above, font=\scriptsize, pos=0.25]{no} (res_idle);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
  \caption{Emergency fault trip and context-preserving recovery flowchart}
  \label{fig:flow-fault}
\end{figure}
