\section{Source code}
\label{app:source}
% =============================================================================

The complete project is available in the team repository \cite{projectrepo}:
\url{https://github.com/nguyenchuyenhoa14-hub/CO3053-BTL2-Washing-Machine}. Directory layout:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=logstyle]
src/include/   washing_machine_config.h, washing_machine_fsm.h, hal_interfaces.h
src/fsm/       washing_machine_fsm.c         portable FSM (core logic)
src/hal/       hal_button_engine.c           debounce + coin pulse decoder
               hal_led_blinker.c             1 Hz / 2 Hz blinker + actuator interlock
               hal_button_gesture.c          click / double / hold decoder
src/app/       wm_single_button.c            gesture -> FSM event mapping
               wm_two_button.c               coin staging front panel
board/weact_h750/
               startup_stm32h750.c, stm32h750vb_flash.ld   startup + linker script
               board_h750.c                  clocks 400 MHz, GPIO, SysTick, USART1
               lcd_st7735.c, gfx.c, ui_render.c, ui_text.c LCD + UART UI
               main_h750.c                   firmware entry point (super-loop)
tests/         test_washing_machine.c, test_hal_engines.c, test_gesture.c,
               test_two_button.c, test_ui_text.c
sim/           sim_interactive.c (CLI simulator), wokwi/ (web simulation)
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent Build and flash: \code{make test test_hal test_gesture} (PC tests), \code{make h750}
(firmware), \code{make flash} (USB-DFU or ST-Link), \code{make preview} (LCD frames on the PC).

\section{Pin configuration}
\label{app:pins}
% =============================================================================

The project does not use STM32CubeMX: peripherals are configured directly through the registers
(\code{board_h750.c}, \code{lcd_st7735.c}) following RM0433 \cite{stm32h7rm}. The resulting
configuration is:

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Register-level pin configuration}
  \label{tab:pinregs}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{lllll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Pin} & \textbf{MODER} & \textbf{PUPDR} & \textbf{AF} & \textbf{Function} \\
    \midrule
    PC13 & 00 input  & 10 pull-down & -- & K1 \\
    PA0, PA1 & 00 input & 01 pull-up & -- & external K0, STOP \\
    PE3  & 01 output & -- & -- & status LED \\
    PE11, PE13 & 01 output & -- & -- & LCD CS, DC \\
    PE12, PE14 & 10 alternate & -- & AF5 & SPI4 SCK, MOSI \\
    PE10 & 10 alternate & -- & AF1 & TIM1\_CH2N backlight PWM \\
    PA9, PA10 & 10 alternate & PA10 pull-up & AF7 & USART1 TX, RX \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
