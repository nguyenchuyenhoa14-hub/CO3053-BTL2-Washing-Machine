\subsection{Operational Lifecycle Analysis}

From the operational perspective of a commercial laundromat, a washing machine cycle represents
a tightly bound physical and financial transaction. To establish a defect-free control architecture,
the system lifecycle is partitioned into four sequential phases:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Monetary Accumulation Phase:} The system begins in an idle, receptive state. The customer
        deposits legal tender tokens ($10\,\text{\textcent}$, $20\,\text{\textcent}$, or $50\,\text{\textcent}$)
        into the acceptor. No mechanical actuators or water valves are energized. The controller merely
        increments an internal balance accumulator. If the user decides to abort prior to starting the wash,
        a dual-press of \code{STOP} returns the entire deposit.
  \item \textbf{Washing Execution Phase:} Upon reaching or exceeding the $50\,\text{\textcent}$ threshold, the
        machine transitions to the ready state. Depressing \code{RUN} locks the door deadbolt, clears the coin
        balance to zero (unconditional forfeit of surplus deposit), and assigns a fixed wall-clock budget
        $T_{\text{cycle}} = 1800\,\text{s}$ ($30\,\text{minutes}$). The controller dynamically steers the drum
        motor between low-speed bidirectional tumbling ($0 \le t \le 1500\,\text{s}$) and high-speed unidirectional
        centrifugal extraction with an active drain pump ($1500 < t \le 1800\,\text{s}$).
  \item \textbf{Operational Suspension (Pause) Phase:} Actuating \code{PAUSE} halts drum rotation and fluid
        drainage immediately, while maintaining the door lock deadbolt to protect the user from escaping hot water
        or splashing suds. Crucially, \emph{the countdown timer belongs to the session slot, not the motor}.
        Wall-clock time continues to decrement monotonically. Resuming via \code{RUN} restores the appropriate
        motor agitation profile with the remaining time budget intact.
  \item \textbf{Finalization \& Termination Phase:} A session concludes either normally when the countdown reaches
        zero ($t = 0$), or abnormally when the user confirms a manual termination by depressing \code{STOP}
        twice within $1.5\,\text{s}$. Upon termination, all actuators are de-energized, the door latch is released,
        and the system resets to \code{STANDBY}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Formal Safety Invariants}
\label{sec:invariants}

To guarantee fail-safe operation under all operating conditions, the software architecture enforces
five mathematical safety invariants. These invariants are verified at compile-time via static assertions
and at runtime through the defensive actuator driver \code{hal_actuator_is_safe()} (validated in unit
test \code{TC-HAL-05}).

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Invariant $I_1$ (Valve-Pump Mutual Exclusion):}
        Under no operational circumstances shall the water inlet solenoid valve and the centrifugal drain pump
        be simultaneously energized:
        \begin{equation}
          \forall t \ge 0, \quad \neg \Big(\text{Actuator}_{\text{valve}}(t) = \text{OPEN} \;\land\; \text{Actuator}_{\text{pump}}(t) = \text{ON}\Big)
          \label{eq:inv1}
        \end{equation}
        \emph{Physical Rationale:} Simultaneous filling and draining causes continuous potable water loss,
        cavitation in the pump impeller, and thermal runaway of the pump motor windings.

  \item \textbf{Invariant $I_2$ (Dynamic Door Deadbolt Safety Interlock):}
        The electromechanical door latch deadbolt must be securely locked whenever the drum motor is active:
        \begin{equation}
          \forall t \ge 0, \quad \Big(\text{Actuator}_{\text{motor}}(t) \in \{\text{AGITATE}, \text{SPIN}\}\Big) \implies \Big(\text{Actuator}_{\text{door}}(t) = \text{LOCKED}\Big)
          \label{eq:inv2}
        \end{equation}
        \emph{Physical Rationale:} Prevents customer access during high-speed rotation ($>800\,\text{RPM}$),
        eliminating the hazard of mechanical entanglement, thermal scalding from hot wash liquor, and sudden room flooding.

  \item \textbf{Invariant $I_3$ (Fail-Safe Quiescence upon Emergency Fault):}
        Whenever an asynchronous safety fault is asserted, all dynamic actuators must transition to their quiescent
        off-state within Kopetz's hard real-time latency deadline $T_{\text{react}} \le 30\,\text{ms}$:
        \begin{equation}
          \forall t \ge 0, \quad \big(S(t) = \text{ERROR}\big) \implies
          \begin{cases}
            \text{Actuator}_{\text{motor}}(t) = \text{OFF} \\
            \text{Actuator}_{\text{pump}}(t) = \text{OFF} \\
            \text{Actuator}_{\text{valve}}(t) = \text{CLOSED} \\
            \text{Actuator}_{\text{door}}(t) = \text{UNLOCKED} \\
            \text{Indicator}_{\text{RLED}}(t) = \text{BLINK } 2.0\,\text{Hz}
          \end{cases}
          \label{eq:inv3}
        \end{equation}

  \item \textbf{Invariant $I_4$ (Unconditional Zero Change Return upon Cycle Start):}
        When the system transitions from the ready state to the running state upon actuation of \code{RUN},
        the accumulated currency balance is cleared to zero without issuing any coin refund:
        \begin{equation}
          \forall t \ge 0, \quad \Big(S(t^-) = \text{READY} \;\land\; e(t) = \text{RUN}\Big) \implies \Big(b(t) = 0 \;\land\; \text{Refund}(t) = 0\Big)
          \label{eq:inv4}
        \end{equation}

  \item \textbf{Invariant $I_5$ (Strict Monotonic Budget Progression):}
        During an active washing cycle, whether in normal agitation, extraction, or temporary pause,
        the remaining cycle budget $t_{\text{remaining}}$ is strictly non-increasing over wall-clock time:
        \begin{equation}
          \forall t_1, t_2 \quad \text{such that } t_1 < t_2 \land S(t_1), S(t_2) \in \{\text{RUNNING}, \text{PAUSED}\}, \quad t_{\text{remaining}}(t_2) \le t_{\text{remaining}}(t_1)
          \label{eq:inv5}
        \end{equation}
        Pausing the machine suspends motor motion, but strictly preserves $t_{\text{remaining}}(t_2) < t_{\text{remaining}}(t_1)$
        under the continuous arrival of 1-second system clock ticks.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{State Space Partitioning: Qualitative Modes vs Quantitative Context}

A direct state-machine representation of an 1800-second timer and variable coin deposits would yield
a state explosion exceeding $1800 \times 100 > 1.8 \times 10^5$ combinatorial states. To ensure strict
MISRA-C compliance and deterministic verification, the system is designed as an \textbf{Extended Finite
State Machine (EFSM)}. The state space is partitioned into:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Qualitative Modes ($S$):} Six discrete behavioral states defining the operational posture
        and visual indicator outputs (Table~\ref{tab:states}).
  \item \textbf{Quantitative Context Tuple ($C$):} A compact, statically allocated data structure:
        \begin{equation}
          C = \big\langle b, t, n, w, f \big\rangle \in \mathbb{N} \times [0, 1800] \times \{0, 1\} \times [0, 1500] \times \{0, \dots, 7\}
        \end{equation}
        where:
        \begin{itemize}
          \item $b$ (\code{coin_balance_cents}): Accumulated financial deposit in cents ($0 \le b \le 9999$).
          \item $t$ (\code{remaining_cycle_sec}): Cycle countdown down-counter in seconds ($0 \le t \le 1800$).
          \item $n$ (\code{stop_press_count}): Number of recorded \code{STOP} pulses within the active window ($n \in \{0, 1\}$).
          \item $w$ (\code{stop_window_timer_ms}): Remaining duration of the double-press detection window ($0 \le w \le 1500\,\text{ms}$).
          \item $f$ (\code{active_error_flags}): Bitmask of currently active hardware and safety faults.
        \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\noindent \textbf{Macro-States vs Micro-States Disambiguation:} The instructor's specification identifies
five qualitative operational macro-modes: Standby, Ready, Running, Paused, and Error. In our formal EFSM,
we introduce the sub-state \st{COLLECTING} to mathematically isolate the condition $0 < \text{balance} < 50\,\text{\textcent}$.
Externally, \st{COLLECTING} presents identical optical indicators to \st{STANDBY} ($\text{RLED}=\text{ON}, \text{BLED}=\text{OFF}$),
preserving 100\% visual and behavioral fidelity with the instructor's assignment slides. Internally, separating
\st{COLLECTING} provides a rigorous mathematical basis for handling pre-cycle session cancellations (Assumption A6)
without polluting the uninitialized idle state.
